REACH Climate–Health EWS
Final GitHub / Streamlit release
Date: 2026-10-02
Release: V6 · Facility spatial gradients + ECMWF/GFS/ERA5 comparison + accessible UI

Core platform retained:
- Zambia: all 116 districts
- Brazil: all 5,572 municipalities
- REACH pilot verification: Senanga, Sinazongwe, Recife and Palmares
- short-, medium-, sub-seasonal and seasonal forecast workflows
- heat, rainfall flood, river-discharge flood, drought and compound-hazard views
- GloFAS, climate drivers, return periods, Stella/SDM hand-off and historical verification

V6 revisions:
- health facilities remain nested under the parent district/municipality
- Brazil facility selector displays real CNES establishment names and no unexplained numeric “CNES type” suffixes
- selected facility drives the detailed forecast calculations using its coordinates
- short/medium range facility comparison samples both ECMWF IFS HRES and NOAA GFS
- heat views add side-by-side facility temperature-gradient surfaces with points and a °C colour scale
- the gradient is explicitly labelled as IDW interpolation of model values sampled at facility points, not the native NWP grid
- rainfall views add side-by-side facility point maps for ECMWF and GFS plus their difference
- point/district/facility time-series analysis adds a 2×2 ECMWF/GFS/ERA5 comparison dashboard
- ERA5 1981–2014 is used as historical climatology/threshold context for future forecasts, not misrepresented as a future observation
- current forecast anomalies are shown relative to the ERA5 day-of-year climatology
- model-comparison values are included in downloadable time-series and selected-data packages
- all analysis tabs and forecast selectors use stronger high-contrast filled styling with bold labels

Scientific boundary:
A facility-specific forecast is a point-specific hazard/exposure signal. It must not be interpreted as operational facility risk unless readiness/access data such as staffing, WASH, electricity, cold chain, service availability or access disruption are explicitly integrated.
