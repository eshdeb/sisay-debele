REACH Climate–Health Early Warning Data Portal
V16 · Forecast-to-Health Impact Outlook
Date: 05 October 2026

Adds a transparent Health impact outlook that links the selected climate/hydrological forecast to health-system access/continuity, maternal and child health service pathways, general population health and WASH/infectious-disease pathways. The screening layer does not fabricate disease cases or live service-utilisation predictions; quantitative health forecasts require calibration against suitable HMIS/surveillance data.
